About My Work

I am interested in open-source software, embedded systems and practical applications of artificial intelligence.

My development workflow often combines Python, TensorFlow and Keras for model development, followed by model conversion and quantization for deployment on resource-constrained devices.

I use LambdaNative and TinyMaix to explore how machine learning models can run locally on mobile and embedded platforms, without depending on cloud-based inference.

My goal is to build accessible, lightweight technology that can address practical challenges, particularly in areas such as agriculture and healthcare.

Disclaimer: The disease classification applications are experimental projects and are not intended to replace professional medical or agricultural diagnosis.
