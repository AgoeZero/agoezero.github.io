About the work

These projects share a common workflow: train a model with Python, TensorFlow and Keras, convert and quantize it for efficient inference, then integrate it into a mobile application built with LambdaNative. TinyMaix provides lightweight model inference on the target device.

Health disclaimer: The Skin Disease Detector is an experimental project for educational and research purposes. It is not a medical diagnostic tool and must not replace advice or diagnosis from a qualified healthcare professional.
